# One-Sheet — design lock (2026-10-15, supersedes Premiere Night's hero and tool)

The owner said Premiere Night still wasn't premium enough: the overall finish, the plain upload area and a first screen without impact. Three gpt-image-2 mockups were made. The owner chose **B, "theatrical one-sheet"**, with one condition: the example film must be prominent. The mockups are in the session scratchpad (`mock-b.jpg`).

## Thesis
The first screen is a film's theatrical poster (a one-sheet), and the film itself is the poster's key art, playing.

## What changes from Premiere Night
- **Hero:**
  - The backdrop is the example film's own opening frame, darkened and softly blurred, full bleed. The light the film throws on the room (`--spill`) is tinted over the backdrop.
  - Left column: an ember kicker in condensed caps, the H1 in **Cormorant Garamond italic** (gilt, large), a gilt rule with a diamond, a poster billing block (condensed caps, alternating small and large words, true facts only, no invented names), the lede, the CTAs and the trust line.
  - Right column: **the example film, large** — as tall as the screen allows (header and beats excepted), in a 1px gilt frame with a 6px mat and a soft warm glow. The beats run under it.
  - On phones the film comes right after a compact title, and the title block follows it.
- **Tool:**
  - Step 1, "Cast your couple": the two photo slots are **screen-test polaroids**. They are cream paper (`--paper`), tilted ±2°, with a strip of tape. A typewriter caption under each reads "SCREEN TEST 001 / ROLE: THE SURVIVOR". The empty state shows an ember "+" on dark film stock.
  - Step 2, "Choose your scene": the scenes are **cinema tickets**. Each ticket is cream paper with notched sides, has a condensed-caps scene name, the scene's loop as a still/clip window and one line of mood. The footer reads "SELECTED ★" or "SELECT". The selected ticket gets a gilt outline.
  - The end title and the main button span both columns underneath.
- **Type roles:**
  - Cormorant italic is used for the hero H1 and the end-title card only. Section H2s stay Archivo condensed caps.
  - Courier Prime is used only on paper (polaroids, tickets) and for slates.

## Tokens (added)
| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--paper` | #E8DFC8 | polaroids and tickets only |
| `--paper-ink` | #2A2620 | text on paper |
| `--frame-glow` | rgba(233, 180, 100, 0.22) | the glow around the hero film's frame only |

Kept: `--void`, `--gilt` (title, rules, film frame, selected ticket), `--ember` (CTA, kicker, selection star, "+"), grain, 2px radii.

## Avoid
- Fake credits (no real artist or studio names).
- Paper surfaces anywhere except the tool.
- Tilting anything other than the polaroids.
- Gold-filled buttons.
- Making the film smaller than the title column.
