## What we make

VideoZombieCouple turns two photos into a short AI film about the two of you. It is not a filter on a selfie and not a template clip with your faces pasted in. Each film is a small story with a beginning, a turn and an ending, and every face in it is made from your photos.

There are two stories today, and you pick one before you upload anything, because each needs different photos.

## How a film is built

Most AI video tools make a whole clip from one prompt in one go. Faces drift, and anything that needs a cut, like a jump from the present to a memory, turns into a blur. We work the other way round.

1. **Key frames first.** From your two photos we draw the moments the film turns on: the standoff and the memory, or the stranger's smile and the first Christmas. We make several versions of each frame and keep the one that looks most like you.
2. **One shot at a time.** Each shot is animated on its own, starting from those frames, so the faces stay put from the first second to the last.
3. **A real cut.** The jump into the memory is a match cut, with the same pose on both sides, not a dissolve between two strangers.
4. **Music and an end title.** The shots are cut to a score timed to the story, and the last frame holds for an end title with a line, a date and your names if you add them.

The result is a vertical MP4 made for phones, TikTok, Reels and Shorts, with a small mark that says it is AI-generated.

## Which film should we make?

Pick the zombie love story if you want something to post: it is short, it turns on one strong moment, and it is the film behind the trend. Pick the pet version if your cat or dog is the other half of the story. Pick the Christmas film if you want something to give: it is longer and gentler, about growing old together, and it works as a present for a partner, your parents or your grandparents, or as a video to send the family instead of a card.

## Honest limits

No AI copies a face exactly, and we won't pretend ours does. Expect a likeness that is close and clearly you, with small differences. Side profiles, small faces, sunglasses and heavy filters make it worse. With bald heads, hats and head scarves the model sometimes adds hair or changes the headwear. In the Christmas film we age you both by decades, which is harder still: a hairline may age its own way.

If the finished film doesn't look like you, remake it once for free while your photos are still kept. The zombie look stays restrained on purpose, with no blood, no wounds and no biting, and the Christmas film touches on memory loss gently, with no hospital and no illness on screen.
