VideoZombieCouple turns two photos into a short zombie love story. One of you has turned. The other can’t pull the trigger, lowers the gun and holds on, and the video cuts to a warm memory of you both before it all went wrong.

## What the tool does

You upload two photos and choose who turns into the zombie. In about a minute you get a free preview: the standoff frame and the memory frame. If you like it, you make the full video. It is a <F k="video.seconds" />-second vertical MP4 at <F k="video.width" />×<F k="video.height" />, with music and an optional end title with your names and a date.

The video is made by AI models from third-party providers. We write the story, pick the best of <F k="candidates.perFrame" /> candidates for each keyframe, and put the shots, color and music together.

## What we are careful about

- Your photos are private and used only to make your video. Originals are deleted within <F k="retention.photosDays" /> days and videos within <F k="retention.videoDays" /> days. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
- Only photos of yourself or of adults who agreed. No minors, no celebrities.
- No gore and no nudity. It is a love story with zombie makeup, not a horror film.
- Every video is fiction. Please don’t use it to fool anyone.
- You see what you are paying for first, and there is no subscription.
