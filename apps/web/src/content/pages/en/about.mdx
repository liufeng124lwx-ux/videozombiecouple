VideoZombieCouple turns two photos into a short zombie love story. One of you has turned. The other can’t pull the trigger, lowers the gun and holds on, and the video cuts to a warm memory of you both before it all went wrong.

## What the tool does

You upload two photos and choose who turns into the zombie. We check the photos for free on our own servers, then you pay and we make the whole film. It is a <F k="video.seconds" />-second vertical MP4 at <F k="video.width" />×<F k="video.height" />: the standoff, the trembling hand, the embrace, the warm memory, then back in the ruins at first light, with music and an optional end title with your names and a date. You don't need to sign up: the email you type at checkout becomes your account.

The video is made by AI models from third-party providers. We write the story, pick the best of <F k="candidates.perFrame" /> candidates for each keyframe, and put the shots, color and music together.

## What we are careful about

- Your photos are private and used only to make your video. Originals are deleted within <F k="retention.photosDays" /> days. Films are kept <F k="retention.guestFilmDays" /> days, or <F k="retention.memberFilmDays" /> days once you confirm your email. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
- Only photos of yourself or of adults who agreed. No minors, no celebrities.
- No gore and no nudity. It is a love story with zombie makeup, not a horror film.
- Every video is fiction. Please don’t use it to fool anyone.
- We don't generate anything for free, so the example films show what you get. If the faces don't come out right, you can remake your film once for free. There is no subscription.
