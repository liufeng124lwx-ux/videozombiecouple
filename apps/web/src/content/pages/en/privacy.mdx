## What we collect

**Photos.** The two photos you upload for a story. You must only upload photos of yourself or of adults who agreed to it.

**What we make from them.** The result of the photo check, and after you pay, the keyframes, the video shots and your finished video. If you add an end title, we keep the names and date you typed.

**Account details.** Your email address, and your Google account ID if you sign in with Google. We never see your Google password. If you buy without signing up, we collect your email at checkout to deliver your film and receipt, and that email becomes your account, with no password. We record whether you have confirmed the email, because that decides how long your films are kept.

**Video credits and payments.** Your video balance and history, and order details from our payment partner: what you bought, the amount, the currency and the country. Card details go straight to the payment partner. We never see your card number.

**Usage and security data.** Which options you chose, how long each step took, errors, and when you visited. To stop abuse, such as too many photo checks, we store a random visitor ID in a cookie, plus scrambled (hashed) versions of your IP address range and of basic device details. We can’t turn these back into your IP address or device.

**Messages.** What you write to us by email.

## Faces, age checks and AI processing

**Face features.** To pick the keyframe that looks most like the people in your photos, we compute face features (numbers that describe a face) from your photos and from each candidate keyframe. This happens in memory only. The features are never stored, and never used to recognize you elsewhere or to train anything.

**Photo check, age check and nudity check.** Before you pay, we check your photos. On our own servers, each photo of a person must show one adult face: we estimate the age of each person, and because these estimates can be off by several years, we refuse a photo if someone looks younger than <F k="age.minEstimated" />. The estimate is used only for that check and is not stored. We also send each photo, through a short-lived private link, to our moderation provider SeeAPI, which tells us whether it contains nudity or sexual content; such photos are refused. This check generates no keyframe or video, and no AI model makes anything from your photos before you pay.

**AI generation.** After you pay, to make keyframes and video shots, your photos are sent to AI models through kie.ai, which runs models from OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Kuaishou (Kling) and ByteDance (Seedance). They process the photos to return the result to us. We never use your photos to train AI.

**Describing head coverings and hair (Christmas film).** For the Christmas film, after you pay, both photos go through kie.ai to Anthropic (Claude) or, if Claude fails or is unavailable, Google (Gemini). The model writes a short description of visible head coverings (such as hats or headscarves), hair, facial hair and glasses, so the people in the film keep their hair, head coverings and glasses. We keep this description with your story, and it is deleted with the photos, within <F k="retention.photosDays" /> days. It is used only to make that film. It is not used to compute face features, and the model providers process the photos only to return the description to us.

## Biometric data

**What it is.** When you upload photos of people, our own servers compute face geometry (a set of numbers that describes a face) from each photo and from each candidate keyframe, and estimate each person’s age. Laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, similar laws in Texas, Washington and Colorado, and the GDPR treat this as biometric data.

**Why we use it.** Only to check that each photo shows one adult face, and to pick the generated frame that looks most like the people in your photos. Never to identify anyone, never for marketing, and never to train anything.

**Consent.** Before every upload, you confirm that you are 18 or older and that each person in the photos is an adult who agreed to be in the film and to this analysis. We record when you confirmed and which wording you agreed to. Films of two people aren’t available in Illinois; the pet version is.

**How long we keep it.** Face geometry exists only in memory while a check runs, for a few seconds, and is then discarded. It is never written to disk, to a database or to a log. The photos it comes from are deleted within <F k="retention.photosDays" /> days. This is our retention schedule: biometric data is destroyed as soon as the check that needed it ends, and never kept beyond that.

**No sale or disclosure.** We never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric data, and we never disclose it to anyone. It never leaves our servers.

**Security.** Photos are stored privately, reached only through short-lived signed links, and sent only over encrypted connections, with the same care as the most sensitive data we hold.

## How long we keep it

- Original photos and keyframes: deleted within <F k="retention.photosDays" /> days. The Christmas film's description of head coverings and hair is deleted at the same time.
- Finished films, and the shots we keep so you can change the end title once: deleted after <F k="retention.guestFilmDays" /> days for accounts whose email isn’t confirmed (for example, if you bought as a guest), and after <F k="retention.memberFilmDays" /> days once the email is confirmed. You confirm it by opening the link in the film email, signing in with a code, or signing in with Google. We email you when the film is ready, with the deletion date, and once the day before. Download your film before then.
- The free remake uses your original photos, so it is only possible while they still exist.
- The free AI zombie filter: the photo you upload and the zombie photo we make from it are both deleted after <F k="photo.keepHours" /> hours.
- Download links: private, and they stop working after <F k="retention.linkMinutes" /> minutes.
- Face features and age estimates: never stored.
- Account and video balance history: until you delete your account.
- Orders: kept for tax and accounting as long as the law requires, without your name or email once you delete your account.
- Story records without photos or videos (options, timings, errors): kept to run limits and fix problems, and stripped of your identity when you delete your account.

If someone uploaded a photo of you without your consent, email us and we will delete it.

We don’t sell personal information and don’t share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
