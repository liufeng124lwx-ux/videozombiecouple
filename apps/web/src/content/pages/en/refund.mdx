Last updated: October 15, 2026

We want you to pay only for videos you are happy with. We don’t generate anything for free, so the example films on the site show what you get, and we check your photos before you pay so you don’t pay for photos we can’t use. This policy explains what happens when a film doesn’t come out right, and when a payment to VideoZombieCouple, operated by Arrayon Inc., can be refunded.

## Faces that didn’t come out right: the free remake

If the faces in your finished film don’t look right, you can remake that film once for free from the story page. It uses the same photos, so it only works while they still exist: photos are deleted within <F k="retention.photosDays" /> days. This is the first thing to try, and it doesn’t use another video from your balance.

## Failed videos

If a video fails to generate, the video goes back to your balance automatically. You don’t need to ask.

## Unused packs

You can get a full refund for a pack if you ask within <F k="refund.packDays" /> days of buying it and you haven’t used any videos from it. When we refund a pack, its videos are taken back from your balance.

If you have used some of the videos in a pack, that pack can’t be refunded.

## Videos you didn’t like

A finished video that generated correctly can’t be refunded, because the AI work has already been done and paid for. Use the free remake first. If something went clearly wrong, such as a broken file, or a remade film that still doesn’t show the people in your photos, write to us and we will look at it.

## How to ask

Email [hello@videozombiecouple.com](mailto:hello@videozombiecouple.com) from the email you used at checkout, with the date of the purchase. We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the card or method you paid with through our payment partner Waffo. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

## Chargebacks

Please write to us before you open a chargeback. We can usually sort it out faster.

## Your legal rights

This policy doesn’t limit any refund rights you have under the law where you live.
